Mechanics

⚔ Flanking: +1 dmg when ally on opposite side (enemies flank too!)

👻 Ghost: Dead players move freely, buff adjacent allies +1 dmg. Cleric can revive!

🛡 Cover hex: -1 incoming damage + regen 1 HP/4 ticks

☠ Hazard hex: 1 dmg when entered

🔥 Lava hex: 2 dmg + burn when entered (appears enc 3+)

🟢 Poison: 1 dmg/tick · ⚡ Stun: Skip action · ❄️ Slow: Can't move

🎯 Combo: Multi-hit same enemy = bonus dmg. Combo chains build multiplier!

🗡 Rogue backstab: +1 dmg from behind · 🔥 Warrior thorns: 1 dmg to melee attackers

💫 Dodge: Shadow Cloak gives 15% passive dodge · 🔥 Berserker: +1 dmg below 50% HP

📱 Touch: Swipe to move · Tap hex to move/attack · Use buttons for skills

Enemies

💣 Bomber: Drops hazard tiles, flees · 🗡 Assassin: Invisible, 3 dmg first strike · 🛡 ShieldBearer: Immune from front, flank it · ✦ Summoner: Spawns minions at range · 🐉 Dragon: Breath line + lava · 🗿 Golem: AoE pound, immune to status

Biomes

🏰 Crypt: Classic dungeon · 🌿 Swamp: Poison + hazards · 🌋 Volcano: Lava + fire · 🏛 Ruins: Walls + cover
